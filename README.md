# Sagar-CareWell-Telemedicine-Care-Platform
> One ecosystem, three interfaces, sharing a single source of truth — 
> appointments, records, vitals, and payments in real time.
Sagar CareWell is a unified telemedicine platform with three integrated portals — Patient, Provider, and Admin — all backed by a single shared database, so scheduling, medical records, vitals, and payments stay in sync across every role in real time.

🔗 **Live Demo:** https://elegant-hotteok-567cfd.netlify.app/

## Portals

### 🧑‍⚕️ Patient App
- Profile with medical history, medications & allergies
- Book appointments with available specialists
- In-app consultation chat
- RPM: heart rate, SpO2 & blood pressure sync
- Digital prescriptions & lab report vault

### 👨‍⚕️ Provider Dashboard
- Patient queue & appointment management
- EHR: clinical notes & diagnosis history
- E-prescribing with digital signatures
- Real-time RPM alerts for abnormal vitals

### 🛠️ Admin Portal
- Doctor license verification & onboarding
- Revenue, platform fees & payout tracking
- Support ticket & dispute resolution

## Highlights
- ⏱ Real-time scheduling shared across every role
- ❤️ Remote patient monitoring with automatic alerting
- 🗄 One Postgres database backs all three portals
